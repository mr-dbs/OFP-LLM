Quick Summary - Return to Help Menu.

Take control of towns for income (move to the flagpole).

Keep a troop presence in a town to greatly increase it's income.

Destroy enemy troops and vehicles to earn bounties.

Change your weapon loadout at a barracks.

Use salvagers (from Repair Depot) to salvage destroyed vehicles for resources.

Purchase different vehicles at the various factories (factory options will appear in Action Menu when close to a factory).

Protect the Mobile HQ at all costs (only armoured vehicle at the beginning of the game).


Base Structures - Return to Help Menu.

Command Center - Only the Commander may use this. Allows upgrades and remote troop/vehicle purchases.

Barracks - Purchase troops and personal weapon loadouts.

Light Factory - Purchase wheeled vehicles. Purchase light armoured vehicles after Light Vehicle Upgrade.

Heavy Factory - Purchase APCs and low-end tanks. Purchase advanced tanks after Heavy Vehicle Upgrade.

Aircraft Factory - Purchase light helicopters and basic transports. Purchase gunships and fixed-wing aircraft after Aircraft Upgrade.

Repair Depot - Purchase salvagers and repair trucks.

Ammo Depot - Purchase ammo trucks. Reloads nearby stationary guns.

Field Hospital - Heal troops. Can choose to respawn at nearest hospital.

M2 Guns - .50Cal MG with 3 levels of protection (gun, post, and fortification).

HE Guns - High Explosive MG with 3 levels of protection (gun, post, and fortification).


Special Vehicles - Return to Help Menu.

Mobile HQ (MHQ) - Needed to construct a base. This is the only armoured vehicle at the beginning of the game and should be left for the Commander. Protect it at all costs...!

Salvager - Can be used to salvage destroyed vehicles for income.

Repair Truck - Can repair vehicles via Action Menu. Drive it up to buildings to repair/contruct them. It can also be used to construct stationary guns, sandbags, and field hospitals.

Ammo Truck - Can rearm vehicles via the Action Menu. It also will rearm nearby stationary guns automatically.


Map Labels - Return to Help Menu.

Team leaders are labeled Alpha-Hotel.

The Commander is labeled Commander.

Towns owned by a side are labeled east or west.

Towns with a target have been taken by each side at least once and may be a potential point of conflict.

Towns with a green circle have friendly troops/vehicles in the vicinity.

Green dots are friendly vehicles.

Green Xs are destroyed vehicles (can be salvaged).

Check the map for your current respawn point if you change the location in the Options Menu.

Alpha - Hotel WP (waypoint) are areas noted by your side and may be of importance. The enemy cannot see these.
